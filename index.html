About

Marketer who builds, runs, and writes.

I'm Mubarak Ibrahim, a digital marketer based in Lagos, Nigeria. My experience is hands-on: I've set up multiple Shopify dropshipping stores across different niches — home goods, pets, tech gadgets, and fitness — handling everything from theme customization and product sourcing to domains, payments, and shipping.

On the traffic side, I've managed TikTok ad accounts end to end: creating ad groups, launching video creatives, monitoring spend, impressions, clicks, and conversions — and connecting ad performance back to real store orders in Shopify.

And because great marketing needs great messaging, I write. My content spans B2B growth playbooks, crypto market news, and corporate ESG analysis — all included below with full text.
