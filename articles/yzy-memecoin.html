Kanye West Launches ‘YZY’ Memecoin on Solana

By Mubarak Ibrahim · News article · Market analysis

Kanye West, also known as Ye, has launched a new memecoin called YZY. The rapper announced the token on August 21 in a post on X, describing it as the start of a “new economy, built on chain.”

The launch drew swift attention. YZY's valuation climbed to $3.2 billion at its peak before cooling to around $1.3 billion. The sharp moves showed how quickly celebrity-backed tokens can rise and fall.

Building a New Economy with YZY Money

YZY is positioned as the base of YZY Money. The ecosystem includes Ye Pay, a crypto payment service, and YZY Card, a global spending tool that works with both YZY and USD Coin. The project calls itself the start of “a new economy” built on-chain. It focuses on payments, commerce, and wider use of digital assets.

The token will be shared through public allocations and liquidity reserves. A portion is locked in long-term vesting under Yeezy Investments LLC to show commitment and transparency. Vesting is carried out on-chain through Jupiter Lock, an audited protocol on Solana.

The launch also introduced an anti-sniping method. The team deployed 25 contract addresses and picked one at random as the official YZY token. It was meant to block bots and give retail buyers a fair chance.

Analysts at Lookonchain highlighted wallets that seemed to know the real contract in advance. One wallet bought YZY before launch and later sold most of its holdings for a profit of more than $1.5 million, raising doubts about equal access.

YZY can be traded on Meteora on Solana. Early trading showed sharp price swings, underlining both excitement and risk. Meanwhile, Solana is trading near $187, up 4% in the past 24 hours. The move shows strong activity on the network.

YZY shows how a celebrity launch can draw instant market attention. Its lasting value, however, will depend on Ye's ability to deliver real payment and commerce tools beyond the early hype.

Meta: Kanye West launched YZY on Solana as part of the YZY Money ecosystem. The token's value briefly hit $3.2 billion before settling near $1.3 billion.
